# RECLAIM website — compliance register

**Status:** researched design constraints, **not legal advice**. Every row needs confirmation by qualified Ontario (and later provincial/US) counsel before launch.
**Research date:** 2026-11-17. Sources were read on that date; "search-only" means the primary page could not be fetched and the point rests on search-engine extracts.

Confidence: **H** = primary text read, unambiguous · **M** = primary text read but application to RECLAIM needs judgement, or search-only · **U** = unverified.

## Changes since the founders' brief (read these first)

1. **CPSO policies were replaced on 2026-11-04.** The former "Advertising" and "Uninsured Services: Billing and Block Fees" policies are now part of CPSO's *Professional Obligations* framework (Practice Management Obligations; Obligations in Practice). Cite the new documents.
2. **Ontario's Consumer Protection Act, 2023 is not in force** (e-Laws, currency 2026-11-12). The 2002 Act governs.
3. **A coaching membership is likely a "personal development service"** under CPA 2002 (health/fitness/diet services paid in advance, over $50). That brings a 10-day cooling-off period, a term of no more than one year, an instalment option and **trust requirements for advance payments when services are not yet available**. This directly affects founding-member pre-sales used as demand tests.
4. **Google Ads (Canada) no longer lists CIPA.** Online pharmacies need LegitScript or NABP certification; telemedicine needs LegitScript.
5. **A physician's name may not appear in advertising for any product or service other than their own medical services** (O. Reg. 114/94 s.6(4)). This constrains how the physician co-founder appears on platform marketing.
6. **The Commitment to the Future of Medicare Act binds "any person or entity", not only physicians.** The platform itself may not charge for, or offer, preferred access to insured services.
