## 0. How to read this document

**Labels (every figure has one)**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Advertised price** | A retail or list price the provider publishes about its own offer. It is what a customer pays, not what the provider pays anyone. |
| **Comparable benchmark** | A third-party estimate, survey, analyst figure, vendor claim about the market, figure from another market (US), or something I calculated from cited figures. |
| **Verified fact** | Stated by a primary authoritative source: a funder, regulator, statute, court or IPC guidance, a published peer-reviewed paper, an SEC filing, or a company's official notice about its own programme. |
| **Unknown** | Not published, not found, or sources conflict. |

**Confidence**
- **H**: the primary publisher's own text, consistent across sources and dated 2025–2026.
- **M**: a secondary source, or the primary text is undated, partly inconsistent, or seen only as a search snippet.
- **U**: unverified, conflicting, stale, or the attribution is uncertain.

**Method limits (please read)**
1. **Pages were not opened.** Direct page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked in this session because the hosts would not resolve: felixforyou.ca, getmaple.ca and benefitsandpensionsmonitor.com failed, so I stopped trying further sites. Every figure below comes from search-engine results that quote or summarise the cited page. No row was confirmed by opening the live page. For that reason even "H" rows should be checked again before external use.
2. **Search was cut short.** The session's web-search limit ran out near the end of the research. Some gaps listed under "Unknown" could probably be filled with more searching: Wellhub per-visit payouts, Planet Fitness and Fit4Less Toronto prices, and Toronto InBody prices.
3. **Rules followed.**
   - No competitor's retail price is shown as a wholesale cost. Partner and aggregator payout rates are given only where a source reports them, and they are labelled as such.
   - No company's ownership or financial structure is inferred from its website.
   - Nobody was contacted, no quote was requested, and nothing was signed up for.
4. **Currencies.** C$ = Canadian dollars. US$ = US dollars, shown as published. No exchange rate was applied.
5. **Tax.** Most Ontario service prices are quoted before 13% HST. Where a source does not say, the tax treatment is unknown.
