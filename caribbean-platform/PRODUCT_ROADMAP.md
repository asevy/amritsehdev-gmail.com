# Product Roadmap

> **Status: v1 (2026-09-09).** The last document of the founding phase —
> a **documentation freeze** is now in effect: new documentation is
> written only when real research exposes a gap. This roadmap lists
> **products, not features**, so everyone builds in the same direction.

## The strategic frame

**The media company is the acquisition channel. The database is the
product.** This is not Forbes Caribbean; it is closer to PitchBook +
Bloomberg + Forbes + LexisNexis for Caribbean capital. Four businesses
feed one core asset:

- The **publication** builds trust.
- The **rankings** build prestige.
- The **awards** build influence.
- The **events** build relationships.
- The **graph** builds enterprise value.

If the website disappeared tomorrow but the ownership graph, the source
archive and the valuation engine survived, the company survives. If the
website is beautiful but the graph is lost, the company disappears.
**The graph is the asset.**

## Build phases (now)

**Phase 1 — Prove the research engine.** The first five fully defensible
family graphs. Not five articles — five datasets, each able to survive
external scrutiny (the reproducibility test). Goddard and Mahfood are
already open; three more follow from the sprint roster.

**Phase 2 — Build the graph.** Forget the website. Everything else is
derived from the graph.

**Phase 3 — Internal tooling first.** Before public readers see
anything, researchers need software that makes them dramatically faster:
fantastic internal claim review, entity resolution, source preservation,
relationship visualization. The public site becomes almost a window into
the internal system.

## Deferred document: the Research Manual

Workflow, not methodology — written only after the validation sprint
teaches the real workflow (per the freeze). Skeleton recorded so it
isn't lost:

> New family discovered → 1. Create Family ID · 2. Open the jurisdiction
> playbook · 3. Resolve person identities · 4. Build the ownership graph
> · 5. Score evidence · 6. Open valuation · 7. Legal review · 8. Right of
> reply · 9. Publish · 10. Schedule monitoring.

The test of the manual: someone joining five years from now becomes
productive without sitting beside the founder.
