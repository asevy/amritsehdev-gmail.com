# Founding Brief — The Master Prompt

> **Status: LOCKED v1 (2026-09-08).** This document is the founding charter
> and no longer accumulates ideas — new thinking goes into the supporting
> documents so the founding thesis stays undiluted. Its hierarchy is
> settled: wealth/business/influence is the core asset; achievement
> broadens relevance; exceptional living creates daily engagement and
> commercial inventory; rankings and awards create IP; Intelligence creates
> the long-term enterprise value.
>
> Preserved 2026-09-08 as the founding statement of the company. The
> strategic sharpening that followed the same day's competitor research is
> recorded in the addendum at the end; it refines emphasis and does not
> replace this brief. The original compact prompt this grew from is kept as
> an appendix for the record.

---

Create a premium Caribbean media, intelligence, rankings and awards company
that becomes the definitive authority on wealth, business, influence,
achievement and exceptional living across the Caribbean.

Think Forbes + Bloomberg + Robb Report + Town & Country + World's 50 Best,
built specifically for the Caribbean.

## Mission

Build a trusted Caribbean institution that documents who owns, builds, leads,
influences and shapes the Caribbean — and the very best the region has to
offer.

The objective is not short-term traffic. Build authority, influence,
proprietary data, prestigious intellectual property and enterprise value that
compound for decades.

Start with the major markets: Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Jamaica,
Bahamas, Cayman Islands, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados and Turks & Caicos,
expanding selectively across the wider Caribbean.

## Wealth & Power

Build a proprietary database tracking:

- Wealthiest Caribbean individuals and families
- Estimated family net worth and changes over time
- Major businesses and ownership
- Investments and transactions
- Family offices
- Business dynasties
- Most influential families
- CEOs and entrepreneurs
- Next-generation family members
- Philanthropy and institutional influence
- Caribbean diaspora wealth
- Major deals, acquisitions and business successes/failures
- Succession and generational wealth transfers

Create flagship annual publications including:

- Caribbean Rich List
- Caribbean Wealth 100
- 50 Most Influential Caribbean Families
- Family Business 100
- Next Generation 40
- State of Caribbean Wealth

Every wealth estimate must clearly distinguish verified facts from estimates
and include methodology and confidence levels.

## Daily Editorial

Publish multiple high-quality stories every day covering important
developments involving Caribbean:

wealth • business • families • society • hotels • restaurants • golf •
real estate • architecture • schools • art • culture • fashion • sport •
entertainment • travel • aviation • yachts • automobiles • wine • rum •
philanthropy • health • wellness • major achievements

**If something materially increases the wealth, prestige, influence,
achievement or global relevance of a Caribbean person, family, company,
institution or destination, cover it.**

This includes everything from a major acquisition or new billionaire to a
Caribbean athlete winning internationally, a major pageant victory, a
restaurant receiving an international award, a new luxury resort opening or a
Caribbean artist setting an auction record.

Use exceptional photography and visual storytelling.

## Reviews, Guides & Rankings

Become the trusted authority on the Best of the Caribbean, including:

**Hotels & Resorts** — Best hotels, new hotels, resorts, suites, spas,
beaches, service, design and private-island resorts.

**Restaurants** — Caribbean 50 Best Restaurants, chefs, new restaurants, wine
programs, bars, local cuisine and dining destinations.

**Golf** — Caribbean 25 Best Golf Courses, resorts, private clubs, course
design, golf communities and tournaments.

**Real Estate** — Exceptional homes, penthouses, villas, developments,
architects, designers, property markets and record sales.

**Education** — Leading private and international schools, tuition,
admissions, facilities, outcomes and annual school guides.

**Society** — Country clubs, yacht clubs, private clubs, charity events,
galas, cultural institutions and important social organizations.

Also cover luxury travel, private aviation, yachts, art and collecting,
automobiles, watches, jewellery, wine, rum, fashion, wellness and exceptional
Caribbean experiences.

## Create Our Own Awards

Build proprietary awards that eventually become among the most prestigious
recognitions in the Caribbean.

Examples:

- Caribbean Person of the Year
- Caribbean Entrepreneur of the Year
- CEO of the Year
- Family Business of the Year
- Deal of the Year
- Caribbean Influence 100
- Next Generation 40
- Hotel of the Year
- Restaurant of the Year
- Chef of the Year
- Caribbean 50 Best Restaurants
- Golf Course of the Year
- Caribbean 25 Best Golf Courses
- Development of the Year
- Architect of the Year
- Private School of the Year
- Artist of the Year
- Athlete of the Year
- Global Achievement Award

Create island-level awards that feed into Caribbean-wide rankings.

Over time, establish an annual Caribbean Awards Gala bringing together
leading families, CEOs, entrepreneurs, developers, hoteliers, chefs,
athletes, artists and cultural leaders.

**Awards and rankings must never be purchasable. Advertising and sponsorship
must remain separate from editorial judgment.**

## Build an Information Asset

Every article should contribute to a growing structured database of:

people • families • companies • ownership • investments • properties •
transactions • executives • relationships • awards • institutions •
estimated wealth • historical rankings

The database should become increasingly difficult for competitors to
reproduce.

## Business Model

Develop diversified premium revenue from:

subscriptions • wealth/business intelligence • proprietary research • annual
reports • luxury advertising • private banks • wealth managers •
law/accounting firms • real-estate developers • hospitality • automotive •
aviation • luxury brands • events • conferences • award ceremonies

Never compromise editorial credibility for advertising revenue.

## Brand

The brand must feel:

authoritative, sophisticated, intelligent, discreet, global, Caribbean and
timeless.

Never become a gossip site or a tacky display of wealth.

The people and institutions covered should eventually regard being profiled,
ranked or awarded as prestigious.

## North Star

Build the institution that journalists, investors, banks, family offices,
businesses and Caribbean leaders cite when they want to understand:

Who has wealth.
Who has influence.
Who is rising.
What is changing.
Who is achieving globally.
And what represents the very best of the Caribbean.

Over 10–20 years, the objective is to become more than a publication.

Become an institution that documents Caribbean success, shapes its
conversation, connects its leaders, confers prestige and owns the most
valuable independent dataset on Caribbean wealth, business and influence.

## Addendum — Strategic sharpening (2026-09-08)

First-pass competitor research (see
[COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md](COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md)) confirmed the brief and
sharpened it in three ways:

1. **Positioning.** Do not build — or describe — "a luxury Caribbean
   magazine". Caribbean Journal already does a good version of that, and the
   "best hotels and restaurants in the Caribbean" territory is occupied.
   Build **the intelligence and media institution for Caribbean wealth,
   business, families, influence and exceptional life — with luxury coverage
   as one highly visible part of it.**

2. **Lead with people, money and power — not hotels.** Hotels, golf,
   restaurants, schools, art, property and travel make the publication
   beautiful and habit-forming and provide enormous advertising inventory.
   But the thing nobody else can easily recreate after ten years is the
   database of Caribbean wealth, families, businesses, ownership,
   transactions, influence and relationships. There is no credible Caribbean
   equivalent of Forbes' wealth infrastructure — that is the whitespace.

3. **The company is a product family, not only a publication.**

   | Line | Product |
   | --- | --- |
   | [Brand] | Free/premium publication |
   | [Brand] Wealth | Wealth rankings |
   | [Brand] Intelligence | Professional database |
   | [Brand] Awards | Rankings and recognition |
   | [Brand] Live | Conferences and summits |
   | [Brand] Society | Invitation-only events |

   The Intelligence product can eventually be sold to private banks,
   investment firms, luxury developers, law firms, recruiters, family
   offices and other institutions that need to understand Caribbean
   capital. That could become much more valuable than advertising.

## Appendix — The original core platform prompt

> The compact version the master prompt grew from, kept for the record.

Build a premium Caribbean media and intelligence platform focused on wealth,
influence, business and elite lifestyle across the major Caribbean markets.

The goal is to become the definitive authority on who owns, builds and
influences the Caribbean — creating a trusted brand, proprietary data and
institutional value that compounds over decades.

Think Forbes + Bloomberg + Robb Report + Town & Country, built specifically
for the Caribbean.

Focus initially on the Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Jamaica, Bahamas,
Cayman Islands, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados and Turks & Caicos.

Core products:

- **Caribbean Wealth Index:** Track and estimate the wealth of major
  individuals and families, with rankings, ranges, methodology, confidence
  levels and regular wealth updates.
- **Influence Index:** Rank the Caribbean's most influential families based
  on business power, institutional relationships, philanthropy, culture,
  social influence and multigenerational reach — not wealth alone.
- **Family Profiles:** Living profiles of important families, their
  businesses, investments, history, next generation, philanthropy and
  lifestyle.
- **Business Intelligence:** Daily reporting on acquisitions, investments,
  expansions, business successes and failures, succession, disputes, major
  developments and movements of capital.
- **Luxury & Society:** High-quality editorial coverage of exceptional homes,
  architecture, private clubs, schools, resorts, yachts, aviation, art,
  wine, travel, fashion, restaurants and significant social events.
- **Annual Reports:** Publish authoritative franchises such as the Caribbean
  Rich List, 50 Most Influential Caribbean Families, Family Business 100,
  Next Generation 40, Luxury Property Report and State of Caribbean Wealth.
- **Proprietary Database:** Build a continuously updated database of
  families, companies, holdings, transactions, relationships and estimated
  fortunes.

Maintain rigorous sourcing and clearly distinguish verified facts, estimates
and editorial analysis. Avoid gossip, sensationalism and pay-to-rank
practices.

The aesthetic should be elegant, restrained, intelligent and authoritative.
The people being covered should consider inclusion prestigious rather than
invasive.

Design the business for long-term value through premium subscriptions,
proprietary intelligence/data, annual reports, events, luxury advertising,
private banking and professional-services partnerships.

North Star: Build the institution that, in 10–20 years, journalists,
investors, banks, family offices and Caribbean leaders cite when they want to
understand wealth, power and influence in the Caribbean.
