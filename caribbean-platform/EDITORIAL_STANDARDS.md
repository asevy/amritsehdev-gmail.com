# Editorial Standards

> **Status: v1.1 (2026-09-09) — principles ratified; founder's review
> applied.** These standards bind everyone, including the founder, from
> before the first publication. A public-facing version is published for
> trust. Exceptions are approved in writing by the standards editor and
> logged. The next test is not writing more rules — it is whether these
> rules survive contact with the first genuinely difficult Caribbean
> fortune.

## The constitution

> Our authority depends on readers believing that our facts are sourced,
> our estimates are intellectually honest, our judgments are independent
> and our coverage cannot be purchased. When commercial opportunity,
> access, relationships or prestige conflict with those principles, the
> principles win.

That paragraph should survive essentially unchanged for 50 years.

## 1. Sourcing — and the two-layer rule

- Facts follow the evidence tiers in
  [DATA_MODEL_AND_METHODOLOGY.md](DATA_MODEL_AND_METHODOLOGY.md) §6:
  registries, filings and court records outrank press; press outranks
  unverified accounts.
- Contested or damaging factual claims require at least two independent
  sources, or one Tier 1 document.
- On-record sourcing is the default and is always preferred.
- **Verified graph vs. research layer.** Nothing supports publication or
  valuation without an approved, sourced claim in the verified graph. The
  internal research layer — tips, hypotheses, unresolved entity matches,
  potential ownership connections — exists precisely so analysts and
  automated systems can work on the unproven; research-layer material can
  NEVER flow automatically into published products. Promotion happens only
  through claim approval.

## 2. AI and automated systems

AI and automated systems may discover sources, extract claims, resolve
entities, monitor filings, calculate valuations, identify anomalies and
propose stories. **AI-generated conclusions are not evidence.**

- Material ownership changes, allegations, private-company valuations and
  consequential editorial claims require human verification against the
  underlying source document before publication.
- Market-price and FX recalculation may be automated, because the
  underlying ownership claim has already been humanly verified. AI
  interpreting a newly discovered trust structure may not.
- Every consequential automated update preserves the exact source document
  and the extracted passage, so an editor can reconstruct how the system
  reached its conclusion.
- One hallucinated ownership interpretation must never be able to move a
  published fortune. The analyst-approval gate in the valuation pipeline
  (DATA_MODEL §9) is a standards requirement, not an engineering
  preference.

## 3. Anonymous sources

Used only when all of the following hold: the information is of genuine
public interest; the source is in a position to know and their identity is
known to the responsible editor; anonymity is necessary to protect them
from real harm; and the information is corroborated independently.
Anonymity is never granted for opinion, insult or score-settling. The
published piece describes the source's basis for knowledge as specifically
as safety allows.

## 4. Wealth estimates

- Estimates are always labeled as estimates, published as ranges with both
  confidence dimensions (evidence confidence and valuation confidence) and
  linked to the methodology version that produced them. No false
  precision, ever.
- The annual Wealth 100 (authoritative, fixed valuation date) and the
  Daily Wealth Index (explicitly mark-to-model) are labeled as what they
  are, everywhere they appear, and never silently substituted for one
  another.
- Verified facts, estimates and editorial analysis are visually and
  verbally distinguished in every profile and ranking.
- No use of stolen, hacked or improperly obtained private financial data.
  Leaked documents of clear public interest are handled case-by-case with
  legal review and never as a routine input.
- Methodology never bends to make a number more dramatic.

## 5. Right of reply

- Subjects receive a genuine opportunity to respond on **material factual
  information and initial wealth estimates**: profile facts, ownership,
  and the basis of an estimate, with corrections accepted before the
  ranking's valuation date. Standard window: five business days for
  profiles (shorter for news, but real). Contact attempts are documented
  and responses fairly reflected.
- **Subjects are not provided their final rank, competitors' estimates,
  scores or unpublished ranking results — for negotiation, approval or
  preview.** Right of reply is about factual accuracy, never about the
  outcome.
- Non-response **may be noted where editorially relevant**; it is not
  boilerplate. A routine confirmation request that goes unanswered does
  not warrant a "did not respond" line that reads as implied accusation.
- Cooperation or hostility NEVER affects placement, scoring or tone.
  Rankings are corrected by evidence, not negotiated.

## 6. Corrections

- Errors are corrected promptly and visibly, with a dated correction note
  on the piece; material corrections are announced, not buried.
- Corrections propagate: the database, affected rankings and any
  syndicated versions are updated; a permanent corrections log is kept.
- No silent edits to substance. Ever.
- **Methodology changes follow the same discipline** (DATA_MODEL §4.15):
  published, explained, versioned — and history is never silently recast;
  original-vintage estimates are preserved alongside any restated series.

## 7. Source-document preservation

Every load-bearing source preserves: original document → retrieval date →
URL/registry identifier → archive copy or hash where legally permissible →
relevant passage/page → reviewing analyst. Web pages disappear and
registries change systems; a challenge in 2038 to an estimate made in 2029
must be answerable from preserved evidence. This is a standards obligation
because it is what makes every published number defensible.

## 8. Conflicts of interest — including the founder

- Staff, contributors and judges disclose financial holdings, family ties
  and business relationships relevant to their coverage; disclosures are
  refreshed annually.
- Recusal is mandatory where a real or reasonably perceived conflict
  exists — from writing, editing, judging or ranking the affected party.
- **The founder firewall, operationalized:** the founder's own
  friendships with covered families, co-investments, outside business
  relationships with covered parties, Society-product relationships and
  gala-table acquaintances are disclosed internally like anyone else's,
  and the founder recuses from affected editorial and ranking decisions.
  Standards prove their value precisely when adhering to them is
  inconvenient for the owner.
- No gifts beyond token value. No paid travel or stays from covered
  parties once anonymous inspection begins; until then, any hosted visit
  is disclosed in the piece and never tied to review or ranking outcomes.

## 9. Material nonpublic information and personal dealing

An ethics line is not enough once Intelligence exists. Institutional
infrastructure, established with securities counsel:

- **Information barriers** between editorial/research and any function
  dealing with markets or clients;
- **A restricted list** of securities where the organization may hold
  material nonpublic information (from interviews, corporate
  relationships, Intelligence customers, embargoes or research);
- **A personal-account dealing policy** — pre-clearance and holding rules
  for staff exposed to MNPI;
- Procedures reviewed by counsel in each relevant jurisdiction, binding on
  everyone with access, the founder included.

## 10. Privacy, harm and security

- Wealth, ownership and influence are legitimate subjects of public
  interest reporting; private life is covered only where legitimately
  public or genuinely material to that public interest.
- **Minors: not named. Default, even when names are publicly available.**
  "The couple have three children" suffices to understand a fortune; a
  child becomes independently coverable only as an adult involved in the
  family's businesses, philanthropy or boards. We never publish where a
  minor child attends school — schools are ranked without connecting
  individual children to them. No wealth attribution to minors.
- **Security:** given regional kidnapping and extortion risk, we do not
  publish home addresses, granular residence locations, travel patterns or
  security arrangements — even when obtainable. The internal database
  enforces the same restrictions on these fields.
- **Homes coverage rule:** homes may be featured with owner consent or
  where ownership and imagery are legitimately public — but editorial
  value never requires publishing information that materially increases
  physical-security risk. No floor plans that expose security layouts, no
  gate or access details, no exact coordinates, no descriptions of
  security systems; consider delaying coverage of newly purchased
  residences. An extraordinary $15M villa can be shown without producing a
  burglar's dossier.
- Requests for de-emphasis on genuine safety grounds are heard seriously;
  requests for flattery are not.

## 11. Political relationships

- Political and institutional influence is covered when it bears on
  business, wealth or public life — carefully, transparently sourced, with
  extra verification and legal review for corruption-adjacent claims.
- The institution endorses no party or candidate, anywhere in the region.
- Government advertising, where accepted, is subject to the same
  separation rules as all commercial relationships and buys no coverage.

## 12. Sponsored content

- Clearly and prominently labeled, visually distinct, and never written or
  assigned by staff who produce rankings, reviews or judged coverage.
- Sponsors receive no preview, veto or influence over editorial content —
  and sponsored content never covers ranking or award outcomes.
- Native advertising that could be mistaken for a profile, review or
  ranking is not accepted at any price.

## 13. Commercial separation (absolute)

The two inviolable rules from
[AWARDS_ECOSYSTEM.md](AWARDS_ECOSYSTEM.md), restated as binding standards:

1. **Nobody can buy an award, a ranking or a favourable review. Ever.**
2. **Commercial relationships never affect eligibility, consideration,
   scoring, placement or editorial coverage. A company does not need to
   advertise, subscribe, attend the gala or purchase a plaque to be
   considered or to win.**

Mechanically: sales staff never see rankings before publication; ranking
and judging teams are firewalled from commercial teams; advertiser status
is invisible in judging materials; use-of-mark licensing is uniform and
never a precondition of the recognition itself; and Intelligence clients
receive data, never editorial influence.

## 14. Style and storytelling

We write about wealth as human stories, not spreadsheets. Our journalism
should be elegant, narrative, curious and deeply reported. Whenever the
reporting supports it, stories begin with people, places, moments and
decisions rather than statistics.

The database supplies the facts; journalism supplies meaning.

We explain how fortunes were created, how families evolved, how businesses
changed places and how power moves between generations.

**Narrative techniques never permit invention.** Scenes, quotations,
motivations, private conversations and descriptive details must be
reported or sourced. We never sacrifice factual certainty for a better
story.

The ideal article is enjoyable enough to read for pleasure and rigorous
enough to cite professionally. Craft guidance — headline forms, story
arcs, voice by section — lives in [HOUSE_STYLE.md](HOUSE_STYLE.md).

## 15. Obituaries & legacies

Legacy pieces meet the same factual standards as everything else, with a
dignity standard: rigorous on what was built and owned, humane in tone,
fact-checked with the family where feasible — without ceding editorial
control.

## 16. Legal review

Mandatory pre-publication legal review for: first publication of any
family wealth estimate; corruption or criminality claims; contested
succession or litigation coverage; and any piece a responsible editor
flags. Review is jurisdiction-aware — defamation regimes differ across the
region and some retain criminal defamation; counsel is engaged per market.

## 17. Enforcement

- A named standards editor owns this document, exception approvals, the
  corrections log and annual review.
- Every editorial hire signs these standards; every judge and inspector
  signs the conflicts policy; everyone with potential MNPI exposure signs
  the dealing policy.
- Violations have consequences up to dismissal; commercial-separation and
  MNPI violations are treated as the most serious categories.
