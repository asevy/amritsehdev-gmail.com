## Execution order

1. **#1 Goddard** — establish the baseline, the claim templates and the
   per-profile time budget on the easiest case.
2. **#3 Mahfood** — first hybrid: one live-priced component, one modeled.
3. **#4 Sabga**, **#2 Corripio** — conglomerate depth, then fully private
   depth (Spanish-language sourcing).
4. **#8 Stewart**, **#10 Lee-Chin** — succession mechanics; diaspora
   eligibility and cross-border attribution.
5. **#9 Vicini** — run the family-web boundary test once claim tooling has
   been exercised.
6. **#7 Beharry** — mainland core case.
7. **#5 Symonette**, **#6 Dart** — the hard cases last, with lessons
   applied; expected to produce the most valuable "where it breaks"
   findings.

## Per-profile deliverables

1. Profile to schema: persons, branches, companies, stakes recorded as
   legal / beneficial / economic / voting where distinguishable, assets,
   transactions, philanthropy, relationships — each as a claim with
   preserved sources and evidence tier.
2. Wealth estimate: range, valuation date, FX date, **evidence confidence
   + valuation confidence**, valuation-state mix (LIVE / MODELLED /
   PROPERTY-MARKED / …), assumption log (debt states, any justified
   marketability adjustment with rationale).
3. Research log: hours, sources used, sources refused/paywalled/absent,
   registry access notes for the market.
4. Gap map: what could not be established and why; whether the gap is
   money, access, or genuine opacity.
5. Story leads that emerged naturally (target ≥3; a measurement, not a
   gate).
6. **Break report:** where the model, the schema or the methodology bent
   or failed on this family — the sprint's most important output.
